GTA6 Uploader

GTA6 Uploader is a private, single-user command-line tool. The owner of one gaming channel on YouTube runs it on their own computer.

What it does

  1. Cuts short vertical clips from gameplay the owner recorded themselves.
  2. Adds captions and an AI voiceover, both generated locally on the computer.
  3. Uploads each clip to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), with a title, description and tags.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and it has no public sign-up.
